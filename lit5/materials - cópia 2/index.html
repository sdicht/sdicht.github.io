Course Materials

Introduction

Read the 📖 Historical Context (from The Norton Anthology of English Literature) to learn about the period.

In class, we compared two excerpts, one from 📖 George Eliot's Middlemarch and another from 📖 James Joyce's Ulysses.

We also read Thomas Hardy's poem 📖 "The Darkling Thrush" and Ezra Pound's 🔗 "In a Station of the Metro".

Poetry

May 5 - World War I Poets

  • Rupert Brooke, 📖 "The Soldier"; Wilfred Owen, "Dulce et Decorum Est"

  • Siegfried Sassoon, 🔗 "Glory of Women"

  • Audio recordings of 🔈 "The Soldier" by Sophie Okonedo; 🔈 "Dulce et Decorum Est" by Kenneth Branagh; and 🔈 "Glory of Women" by anonymous

If you want to know more, watch the movie 📼 Benediction about Siegfried Sassoon.

Here are the slides I used in class. 📊 Slide Deck

May 12 - Diction

  • Shira Wolosky, The Art of Poetry - 📄 "Individual Words"
  • Henry Reed, 🔗 "The Naming of Parts", including 🔈 audio readings
  • W. B. Yeats, 🔗 "The Lake Isle of Innisfree"
  • Philip Larkin, 🔗 "This Be the Verse"

May 19 - Syntax

  • Shira Wolosky, The Art of Poetry - 📄 "Syntax and the Poetic Line"
  • W. B. Yeats, 📄 "Leda and the Swan" (also inside chapter 2). Listen to 🔈 a reading and another by 🔈 Jeremy Irons. Learn about 🔗 the myth.
  • Seamus Heaney, 📄 "Digging". Read by 🔈 the author
  • Ted Hughes, 🔗 "The Thought Fox". Read by 🔈 the author and an 📺 interview about it

  • Dylan Thomas, 📄 "Do Not Go Gentle Into That Good Night". Read by 🔈 the author, by 🔈 Michael Sheen, and my favorite, the great 🔈 Richard Burton

  • ✏️ In-class activity

May 26 - Images

  • Shira Wolosky, The Art of Poetry - 📄 "Images - Simile and Metaphor"
  • T.S. Eliot, 🔗 "The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock" with readings by 🔈 Alec Guinness and 🔈 Anthony Hopkins. Check out the 🔗 comic book adaptation.

  • William Shakespeare, 🔗 Speech: “To be, or not to be, that is the question” with readings by 🔈 Kenneth Branagh, 🔈 Richard Burton, 🔈 Lawrence Olivier, 🔈 David Tennant, and 🔈 Andrew "Hot Priest" Scott

  • ✏️ In-class activity

June 2 - Voice

  • Philip Larkin, 📖 "Church Going", read by 🔈 the author

  • Ted Hughes, 📖 "Hawk Roosting", read by 🔈 the author
