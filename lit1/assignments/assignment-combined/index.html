Part 1: Initial Identification (Group Work)

For each mystery character below, work with your group to determine: 1. Who are they? (profession/social role) 2. What clues reveal this? (specific words/phrases) 3. What sounds positive? What sounds negative? 4. Modern equivalent: If this person existed today, who would they be?

GROUP A: Religious Figures

Mystery Character 1

She wolde wepe, if that she saugh a mous
Kaught in a trappe, if it were deed or bledde.

Of smal coral aboute hire arm she bar
A peire of bedes, gauded al with grene,
And theron heng a brooch of gold ful sheene,
On which ther was first write a crowned A,

Mystery Character 5

A learned man he was, a clerk,
That unto logyk hadde longe ygo.
As leene was his hors as is a rake,
And he nas nat right fat, I undertake,
But looked holwe, and therto sobrely.
Ful thredbare was his overeste courtepy,
For he had not yet gotten hym no benefice,
Of studie took he moost cure and moost heede.
And gladly wolde he lerne and gladly teche.

GROUP C: Commerce & Trade

Mystery Character 6

Ther was a man with a forked berd,
In mottelee, and hye on horse he sat;
His resons he spak ful solempnely,
Sownynge alwey th' encrees of his wynnyng.
Ther wiste no wight that he was in dette,
So estatly was he of his governaunce
With his bargaynes and with his chevyssaunce.
For sothe he was a worthy man with alle,
But, sooth to seyn, I noot how men hym calle.

And though that he were worthy, he was wys,
And of his port as meeke as is a mayde.

Part 4: Synthesis Question (Written Response)

Choose either the Prioress, Monk, OR Friar and write a paragraph (6-8 sentences) explaining:

  • What specific details reveal the gap between their religious duties and actual behavior
  • How Chaucer uses language that sounds positive but actually criticizes
  • What this character reveals about the medieval Church
  • How we might see similar hypocrisy today

Use at least TWO specific quotes from the Middle English text in your answer.
