# Assignment 0: The Adventure of English - Viewing Assignment

## Instructions

Watch the first two episodes of the documentary “The Adventure of English” and write a **1-2 page response** about what you found interesting.

**Episode 1:** Birth of a Language - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRxKMgXsl9c  
**Episode 2:** English Goes Underground - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlGD7y8Ngjo

## What to include:

**Pick 2-3 things that stood out to you:**

- Specific moments, examples, or stories from the episodes
- Something that surprised you about how English developed
- Connections between what you saw and modern English (or other languages you know)
- Whether any of this was familiar from previous classes

**Questions to think about:**

- How did invasions and conquests change the language?
- What does it mean that English “went underground” after 1066?
- Why do we have so many different words for the same things?

## Guidelines:

- 1-2 pages, informal but thoughtful
- Focus on what genuinely interested you rather than trying to cover everything
- Feel free to include questions the documentary raised for you
