# Othello Act 1 Activity

## **Who Is Othello?**

Act I gives us two very different pictures of Othello: the one drawn by other characters, and the one he draws of himself. How do they compare?

1. Search **1.1** for lines where another character describes Othello — his body, his race, his position, his actions. Now, look for **1.2 and 1.3** for lines where Othello describes himself or his situation. Find **two or three lines** you think are most revealing and mark them.

2. Discuss:

    a. How do the characters in 1.1 describe Othello? How does Othello describe himself? What does he conspicuously *not* address? 
    
    b. Othello is a military commander and a stranger in Venice simultaneously. Does his behavior in 1.2 and 1.3 reflect awareness of both roles? Does his self-presentation seem calculated, natural, or both?
    
    c. By the end of Act I, do you trust the portrait painted by Iago and Roderigo, the one Othello paints of himself, or neither?

3. Plenary

Prepare **one sentence** that tracks the development of the characterization of Othello from the beginning of act 1 to the end. Anchor your answer to **at least one specific word or phrase** you found in the text.
